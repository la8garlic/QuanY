# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

This policy applies to QuanY issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions. People of all experience levels are welcome to ask questions, challenge a design and propose changes. Discuss code, evidence and user needs.

Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats and unauthorized disclosure of private information or manuscripts are unacceptable. Maintainers may request changes, hide or remove content, lock a conversation or restrict participation according to severity. Use GitHub's reporting control on the content to report abuse. Avoid repeating private details in a public thread. [GitHub reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
